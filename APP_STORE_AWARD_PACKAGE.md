## Editorial Pitch

DueProof turns scattered receipts, cards, warranties, and claim deadlines into calm, actionable claim cards. It helps people see what value is at risk, keep proof ready, and act before a return window, reimbursement deadline, rebate, or renewal slips away, all without accounts, ads, tracking, or a backend.

People lose money and benefits because proof and deadlines are scattered. DueProof makes those expiring rights visible.

## Five-Sentence Founder Story

DueProof started from a common frustration: losing money because the receipt, warranty, gift card, or deadline was somewhere else when it mattered. The app treats every return, reimbursement, renewal, and proof document as an expiring right worth protecting. It is intentionally local-first because proof and financial details should not require an account or tracking. The design goal is calm urgency: enough clarity to act in time, without turning everyday deadlines into stress. DueProof exists to help people recover value they already have a right to claim.
