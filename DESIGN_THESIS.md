# DueProof Design Thesis

App name: DueProof

Tagline: Keep proof. Beat deadlines.

Product thesis: DueProof makes invisible deadlines visible by turning expiring rights into private, actionable claim cards.

## Design Principles

1. Proof first
   The app exists to protect evidence and deadlines.

2. Quiet urgency
   DueProof should communicate urgency without panic.

3. One obvious next action
   Every important screen should make the next action clear.

4. Local by default
   Privacy is not a setting. It is the product.

5. Apple-native restraint
   Use familiar platform patterns, not novelty for novelty's sake.

6. Recover value
   The user should feel that the app helps them save or recover money.

7. Accessible from the start
   Every experience must work with Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, dark mode, high contrast, reduced motion, and large tap targets.

## Anti-Principles

- No dashboards full of noise.
- No fake finance-app complexity.
- No generic receipt-storage UI.
- No unnecessary charts.
- No decorative glass everywhere.
- No interactions that look cool but slow the user down.
- No misleading privacy claims.

## Signature Object

The Claim Card is DueProof's central design object. It should communicate what is at risk, the value, the deadline, proof status, reminder status, and the next action without forcing the user to inspect multiple screens.

## Product Center

DueProof helps users keep proof and beat deadlines for returns, gift cards, warranties, reimbursements, rebates, subscriptions, renewals, and important documents. The home screen should answer: what money is at risk, what needs attention soon, what has already been recovered, and what should be added next.
