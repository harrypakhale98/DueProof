The problem

The money you can recover usually has a deadline.

Receipts sit in photos, return policies hide in email, gift cards expire quietly, and reimbursement windows close without warning. DueProof turns those loose pieces into clear claim cards with proof, dates, value, reminders, and the next action in one place.

Designed to feel like it belongs on iPhone.

  • Dashboard for money at risk, urgent claims, active claims, and recovered value.

Privacy

Private is the product, not an upgrade.

DueProof does not use a DueProof account, ads, analytics, tracking, telemetry, a DueProof-operated backend, or a remote push notification service. Claim data is stored locally by default, with optional private iCloud sync controlled by you.

Details

Built for the real edge cases.

Does DueProof file claims for me?

No. DueProof helps organize evidence, reminders, action links, and message drafts. You still verify requirements and submit through the right merchant, employer, insurer, provider, or agency.

What happens to proof photos?

Proof photos are copied into protected app storage. Readable text can be saved locally for search. Proof files stay on device unless you share, export, or enable private iCloud sync.

Can I use it without iCloud?

Yes. DueProof is local by default. iCloud Sync is optional and uses your private iCloud database when enabled.

What categories does it support?

Returns, gift cards, warranties, reimbursements, rebates, subscriptions, renewals, documents, and other claim deadlines.
