Email support

What to include

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The DueProof app version from Settings.
  • A short description of what you were doing.
  • Whether the issue involves reminders, iCloud Sync, App Lock, import, export, proof files, widgets, or Smart Fill.

Quick help

  • Reminders depend on iOS notification permission, Focus settings, and system scheduling.
  • iCloud Sync is optional and applies on the next app launch after you change the setting.
  • App Lock disables Spotlight claim search so private details do not appear outside DueProof.
  • Smart Fill suggestions are meant to be reviewed before saving a claim.

Claim note

DueProof helps organize proof, deadlines, reminders, action links, exports, and message drafts. It does not file claims for you, guarantee reimbursement, or replace official deadlines and requirements.
